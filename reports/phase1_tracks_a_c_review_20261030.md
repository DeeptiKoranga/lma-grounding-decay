## Limits that remain real

The original target counts are met: 1,095 news documents and 61 official documents. The exact named source mix is not: Business Standard, RBI and PIB remain absent; ET supplies 870 of 1,095 news documents and OEA supplies ten official documents. This was already adopted as a scoped handoff deviation on 27 October. The present review confirms the deviation rather than silently changing the original plan.

The six-series store is implemented and source-reproducible. Its four sparse histories cannot establish every proposed event date or daily threshold. The October retrospective snapshot remains separate. No missing historical value, availability date or anchor confirmation has been invented.

The completed C2 pilot does not mean the chosen model is accurate on every task. Qwen3-8B scored only 1/20 strict extractions and 2/20 tool choices; it tied at a 61% five-task mean and was chosen by the pre-recorded lower-latency rule. All failure outputs and error analysis remain available. The real controller is deterministic; verification and source guards remain required.

The existing Track A record acceptance rests on the user’s independence/blinding attestations and unchanged original label provenance. The safety review is explicitly by an assistant. Software cannot convert those facts into independently observed human conduct or experimental truth.

This review closes the requested Phase 1 A/C inspection and repairs. It does not declare the entire mid-submission or later study complete: Track B, independent experimental gold/support review, Cohen’s κ, headline UER and final release acceptance remain separate plan items. Model-generation errors and baseline quotation failures remain visible evaluation outcomes. Passing these checks does not guarantee that every future input and environment will be error-free.
